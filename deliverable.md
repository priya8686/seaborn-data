# Titanic Seaborn Project - Deliverable

## Plots and short explanations
1. overall_survival.png — Overall survival count. Conclusion: X% survived.
2. survival_by_sex.png — Survival rate by sex. Conclusion: females had higher survival.
3. survival_by_class.png — Survival by passenger class. Conclusion: higher class => higher survival.
4. age_by_survival.png — Age distribution vs survival. Conclusion: younger passengers had higher survival.
5. survival_class_sex.png — Interaction of class and sex. Conclusion: female advantage persists across classes but stronger in higher classes.

## Data & methods
- Dataset: titanic.csv
- Tools: Python, pandas, seaborn, matplotlib
